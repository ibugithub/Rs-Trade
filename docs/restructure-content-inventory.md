# Information we need from you

Updated: 2026-10-04.

This file answers: **What words, pictures and business details do we need to finish the website?**

You do not need to provide everything at once. We can start the design while you collect the information.

## What we already know

- The website will mainly use English.
- Your customers include businesses, wholesalers, importers and individual buyers.
- You offer sourcing, supplier verification, QC and full door-to-door import.
- Ready Stock will start with about 5-10 selected products.
- Customers will confirm orders through WhatsApp or phone.
- There will be no online payment for now.

**Example:** A few sentences explaining what your company does and how your team helps customers. We can help turn your notes into English website text.

- [ ] Phone number.

## 4. Ready Stock products

For each of your first 5-10 products, provide:

- [ ] Product name.
- [ ] Real product photos.
- [ ] Short description and important specifications.
- [ ] Minimum order quantity (MOQ).
- [ ] Price and currency, or a decision to show Request Price.
- [ ] Available quantity.
- [ ] Delivery information.

**Example:** If a product has MOQ 1, an individual buyer can order one piece. If it has MOQ 20, the website should clearly say the minimum is 20 pieces.

The example products and ratings in the current code are not confirmed as your real stock.

## 5. Service details

We know the four services. We still need the details of how you carry them out.

| Service | What we need to know |
| --- | --- |
| Product Sourcing | What the customer sends you, what steps you take and what information you return |
| Supplier Verification | What checks you actually perform and what report the customer receives |
| QC | What you inspect, how you report the results and who approves shipment |
| Door-to-Door Import | What the quoted price includes, payment arrangements, expected delivery time, delivery areas and any products you cannot handle |

**Door-to-door handling is already confirmed.** You have said RS Trade will handle the full process. We are only waiting for the detailed pricing and delivery terms.

## 6. Photos, videos and completed work

For Our Work and the home page:

- [ ] Real factory visit photos or videos.
- [ ] Real inspection photos or videos.
- [ ] Examples of completed work, with a short explanation.
- [ ] Inspection report examples that are suitable to share.
- [ ] Genuine customer feedback, if available.

## 7. Common customer questions

Tell us the questions customers usually ask and your actual answers.

**Examples:**

- Can I order one piece?
- What information do you need to source a product?
- What does the inspection report include?
- How do I confirm a Ready Stock order?
- How are shipping costs calculated?

We can draft answers from confirmed information and let you review them.

## 8. Business rules

For the Terms and product/service pages:

- [ ] How quotations and orders are confirmed.
- [ ] Payment arrangements after WhatsApp/phone confirmation.
- [ ] Whether orders can be cancelled and under what conditions.
- [ ] Return/refund arrangements, where applicable.
- [ ] What customer information you keep and how long you keep it.

No online payment integration does not remove the need to explain how customers pay after confirming an order.

Google Search Console helps you see how your website appears in Google searches. Visitor tracking helps you understand which pages and contact buttons people use.

Start with these four things:

2. A short company introduction.
3. Real contact details.
4. Your first Ready Stock products.

We can use clearly marked temporary text during design. Before launch, we will replace it with approved information or leave the unfinished section out.
